# Results: Ryzen 7 4800H, 10M entries

Baselines are the Go maps closest to the post's: google/btree and tidwall/btree
for std's `BTreeMap`, zhangyunhao116/skipmap for crossbeam-skiplist,
`sync.RWMutex` + google/btree for `RwLock<BTreeMap>`, Go's `map` and
xsync.Map as unordered references. Lookups are half hits, half misses.
Batched: `GetMany` 64 keys a call, `InsertMany` 16 entries a call.
